    Submit a support ticket

    Describe your issue in as much detail as you can — the more we know upfront, the faster Level 1 can diagnose it. You'll receive a ticket reference and an email acknowledgement once it's logged.

    What happens next

    Your request is logged with a unique Incident ID, categorised and prioritised, then assigned to a Level 1 technician. If it can't be resolved at Level 1, it's escalated with the diagnostic evidence already attached — you don't have to repeat yourself.
