    How to use the portal

    Three steps to get help, in the order the service desk actually works through them.

    1. Log a ticket

    Use the Submit a Ticket page to describe your issue. You'll get a reference number straight away.

    2. Check the knowledge base

    Common issues — Wi-Fi, printing, access requests — often have a documented fix you can try first.

    3. Track progress

    Your technician updates the ticket status as they work; escalations to Level 2/3 happen automatically when needed.
